About VoxShield AI

VoxShield AI is an AI-powered voice security platform designed to detect synthetic and cloned voices in real-time. Built for Smart India Hackathon 2025.

Our detection engine combines a pretrained AASIST deep learning model (99.2% accuracy on ASVspoof benchmarks) with interpretable acoustic signal analysis — pitch stability, breath detection, silence naturalness, and spectral cutoff analysis.

Privacy First: Zero audio storage. No transcription. All analysis happens locally. Your voice data is never stored or transmitted.
